# Privacy Policy for Lycoris Chrome Extension

Last updated: August 24, 2026

Lycoris ("we", "our", or "the extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding data collection, usage, and storage.

## 1. Information Collection and Use
Lycoris does not collect, transmit, or store any of your personal data, browser history, or generated images on any remote servers. 
- All image generations are handled directly inside your active ChatGPT (DALL-E 3) or Gemini (Imagen 3) tabs.
- All image processing (resizing, format conversion, background removal) is done locally inside your browser using the Chrome Offscreen Canvas API or sent to your own configured background removal API.

## 2. Permissions Used
The extension utilizes the following permissions strictly for local features:
- **storage**: To save your export preferences (such as quality and resolution).
- **activeTab / scripting**: To read and download generated images from ChatGPT or Gemini.
- **downloads**: To save the finished ZIP files directly to your device.
- **offscreen**: To run image processing without locking your browser thread.

## 3. Data Sharing
We do not sell, trade, or transfer any user information to third parties. All stored settings remain entirely local to your Chrome browser profile.

## 4. Changes to This Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.

## Contact Us
If you have any questions, you can contact us at the publisher email listed in the Chrome Web Store.
